(FAQs)

Here's what you need to consider before partnering with us.

Founder-led businesses that have built a real reputation but whose brand and website have not kept up with their growth — studios, architecture practices, e-commerce brands, and professional services.

Abood leads every engagement — strategy, creative direction, and your main point of contact from the first call to launch. A small, carefully chosen team of collaborators supports on design, 3D, and development when the scope calls for it.

Most brand and website projects run four to eight weeks end to end. Timelines flex with scope, but we set milestones from day one so there are no surprises.

A shared project space holds the timeline and deliverables. You get async video updates plus weekly messages on WhatsApp or email, and live calls are reserved for decisions and milestones.

A short conversation about what you're building, a signed proposal, and the initial deposit. Onboarding is deliberately quick so we can get to the work.

Not always. If your identity still represents who you are, we build on it and focus on the website. If it no longer reflects the quality of your work, we'll say so honestly and fix it first.
