(About)

Dentistry without the lectures.

A small clinic on 97th Street, built around people who would rather be anywhere else.

NUV Dental opened in 2019 with three treatment rooms and one rule: nobody gets talked into treatment they do not understand. We are a general practice, which means most of what you need — cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canals, whitening, aligners and implants — happens here, with the same faces each visit.

We keep the days deliberately uncrowded. Appointments are longer than you may be used to, running late is rare, and the person who greets you at the desk is the person who answers the phone.
