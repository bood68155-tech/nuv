FAQs

Before you
partner with us.

Here is what you need to consider before we start working together.

Who do you usually work with?

Founder-led businesses that have built a real reputation but whose brand and website have not kept up with their growth. That includes agencies, studios, professional services, commerce brands and early-stage products.

Who actually does the work?

Abood leads every engagement — strategy, creative direction and your main point of contact from first call to launch, supported by a small, carefully chosen team of collaborators on design and development.

How long does a project take?

Most brand and website projects run four to ten weeks end to end. Timelines flex with scope, but we set milestones from day one so there are no surprises.

How do we stay in touch?

A shared project space holds timelines and deliverables, and you get async video updates plus weekly messages on WhatsApp or email. Live calls are reserved for decisions and milestones.

What does it cost?

Websites start from $2,500 and most projects land between $4,000 and $12,000 depending on scope. We will send a tailored proposal after the discovery call — no fixed packages, no padded retainers.

Do I need a full rebrand?

Not always. If your identity still represents who you are, we build on it and focus on the website. If it no longer reflects the quality of your work, we will tell you honestly and fix it first.

What happens after launch?

You get 90 days of hands-on support, plus documentation and a walkthrough so your team can update the site themselves. Ongoing care plans are available if you would rather we handle it.
